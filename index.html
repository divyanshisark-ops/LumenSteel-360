How It Works

Three seamlessly integrated layers of hardware built into one premium everyday flask.

1. Real-Time Tracking

An internal low-power tracking chip interfaces directly with smartphone networks so you can instantly map coordinates if left behind.

2. UV-C Self-Cleaning

The specialized cap automatically emits targeted purifying ultraviolet rays every two hours, eliminating biological odors and keeping things clean.
